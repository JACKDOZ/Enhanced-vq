# Enhanced-vq 0.9.4 — Update Notes

New **Warrior** tab (mob hunting + raids), new menu with quick access to **Bestiary** and **Progress**, and performance improvements.

---

## ⚔️ "Warrior" tab — new

- New tab (axe icon) for hunting creatures: every Bestiary mob has its own quest chain based on kill count.
- Full coverage: all 44 Bestiary creatures now have their own quest and reward — from the most common (zombie, skeleton, spider) to special variants (mounted horsemen, Chicken/Spider Jockey, Piglin Brute) and all 6 bosses (Elder Guardian, Ravager, Warden, Wither, Dragon, and now also the Creaking).
- Most mobs have 3 tiers (1 / 5 / 10 kills), with scaling rewards and a custom mob-head trophy at the third tier.
- Genuinely rare variants (mounted horsemen, Chicken/Spider Jockey) use smaller goals (1 / 2 / 3) so they stay achievable, given how low their spawn chance is.
- Bosses only require a single kill, with a higher-value trophy.
- Thematic rewards, some otherwise hard to obtain: Wither Skeleton Skull, Iron Horse Armor, a full Shulker Box, an enchanted Bow (Power III), and the "Lava Chicken" music disc for defeating a Chicken Jockey.
- Also adds a Raid quest chain (levels I through V).
- Fully automatic tracking: every kill registers itself during normal play, no extra steps needed.

## 📖 New menu — Bestiary and Progress

- New icon in the book that opens a menu with two sections: **Bestiary** and **Progress**.
- **Bestiary**: a catalog of all 44 creatures in the game. Each one starts hidden (silhouette) and reveals itself the first time you defeat it, showing its name, first appearance, description, trivia, a tip, and its drops. Mobs with dedicated artwork (zombie, skeleton, enderman, Zombified Piglin, and more being added over time) get a full illustrated page instead of the simple scroll layout.
- **Progress**: a single-screen summary of every category (Main, Explorer, Miner, Builder, Engineer, Mage, Warrior, Achievements) — icon, color-coded name, and a completion-percentage bar for each, without needing to open each tab individually.

## ⚡ Performance improvements

- Internal rendering optimizations for the book (less repeated work when drawing animated icons and long quest lists), making the interface feel smoother, especially in the Bestiary and Warrior tabs with many entries on screen.
